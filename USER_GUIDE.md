## 1. Home Page

The home page is the first thing you see when you visit the website.

**What you'll find:**
- **Hero Banner** — A welcome section with a brief introduction to the PIO
- **Active Disaster Alerts** — If there are any active weather or emergency alerts, they appear at the top as colored banners (yellow for advisories, red for warnings/critical)
- **Latest Announcements** — The 6 most recent municipal notices and advisories
- **Upcoming Events** — The next 4 community events happening in Naujan
- **Quick Links** — Shortcut buttons to key pages (File a Complaint, Submit Feedback, View Services, etc.)

**How to navigate:**
- Use the **navigation bar** at the top to go to any section
- On mobile, tap the **☰ menu icon** to open the navigation menu
- Scroll down to explore featured content
- Click any announcement or event card to see its full details

## 2. Announcements

**URL:** [/announcements](https://pio-naujan.onrender.com/announcements)

This page lists all official municipal announcements — notices, advisories, ordinances, resolutions, and job postings.

**How to use:**
1. Browse the announcement cards displayed on the page
2. **Filter by category** — Use the category filter dropdown to narrow results (e.g., "Ordinance", "Advisory", "Job Posting")
3. **Click on any announcement** to read the full details, including images and the issuing department
4. **Pagination** — Use the page numbers at the bottom to navigate through older announcements
5. Pinned (important) announcements always appear at the top

**What each card shows:**
- Title and a brief excerpt
- Category badge
- Date published
- Featured image (if any)

## 7. Disaster Updates (DRRM)

**URL:** [/disaster-updates](https://pio-naujan.onrender.com/disaster-updates)

Stay informed about weather conditions, calamity advisories, and emergency updates.

**How to use:**
1. Active alerts are displayed with **color-coded severity levels:**
   - 🟢 **Information** — General updates, no immediate danger
   - 🟡 **Advisory** — Be alert, potential hazard in the area
   - 🟠 **Warning** — Take precautions, danger is likely
   - 🔴 **Critical** — Immediate action required, severe danger
2. Each alert shows:
   - Title and description
   - Affected areas
   - Date and time posted
   - Recommended actions
3. Check this page regularly during typhoon season (June–November)

## 9. Filing a Complaint

**URL:** [/complaint](https://pio-naujan.onrender.com/complaint)

Citizens can file complaints about municipal services, infrastructure, or other concerns.

**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to the **File a Complaint** page
2. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Full Name** — Your complete name
   - **Email Address** — Where you'll receive updates (optional but recommended)
   - **Phone Number** — Alternative contact
   - **Address & Barangay** — Your location
   - **Category** — Select the type of complaint (e.g., Infrastructure, Health & Sanitation, Peace & Order)
   - **Department** — Select the relevant department (optional)
   - **Subject** — Brief summary of your concern
   - **Description** — Detailed explanation of your complaint
3. Click **Submit Complaint**
4. You will receive a **Tracking Code** (e.g., `NJN-2026-XXXX`)
5. **⚠️ Save your tracking code!** You need it to check the status of your complaint later

## 10. Tracking a Complaint

**URL:** [/track-complaint](https://pio-naujan.onrender.com/track-complaint)

Check the current status and updates of a previously filed complaint.

**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to the **Track Complaint** page
2. Enter your **Tracking Code** in the search box (e.g., `NJN-2026-XXXX`)
3. Click **Track**
4. You will see:
   - **Current Status** — Pending, In Progress, Resolved, or Closed
   - **Complaint Details** — Your original submission
   - **Updates** — Any responses or actions taken by the relevant department
5. If no result is found, double-check that you entered the correct tracking code

## 14. Tips & FAQ

### General Tips
- **Bookmark the site** for quick access: `https://pio-naujan.onrender.com`
- The site works on **mobile phones, tablets, and desktops**
- If the page takes a moment to load on your first visit, that's normal — the server wakes up after periods of inactivity
- All pages are accessible without logging in

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to create an account to use the website?**
A: No. All public features — announcements, events, documents, complaints, and feedback — are available without an account.

**Q: How do I file a complaint?**
A: Go to [/complaint](https://pio-naujan.onrender.com/complaint), fill in the form, and submit. Save the tracking code you receive.

**Q: I lost my complaint tracking code. What do I do?**
A: Contact the PIO directly at **publicinformationoffice02@gmail.com** or call **0968-245-2430** with your name and complaint details.

**Q: How long before my complaint gets a response?**
A: Processing times vary depending on the complaint type and department. You can check the status anytime using your tracking code.

**Q: Can I download government documents?**
A: Yes. Go to the [Documents](https://pio-naujan.onrender.com/documents) page and click the download button on any document.

**Q: Is the website available 24/7?**
A: Yes, the website is accessible at any time. However, PIO staff responds to complaints and feedback during office hours (Mon–Fri, 8 AM – 5 PM).

**Q: The website is loading slowly. What should I do?**
A: The first visit may take 30–60 seconds if the server was inactive. Refresh the page and it should load normally after that.
